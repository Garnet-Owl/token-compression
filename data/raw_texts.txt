ATP is the primary energy currency of the cell. Cells produce ATP through various metabolic processes. During photosynthesis, plants produce ATP to power the Calvin cycle. In animals, ATP is produced during cellular respiration. When a cell needs energy, it breaks down ATP into ADP. This process releases energy that the cell can use. Maintaining a constant supply of ATP is essential for all living things. Scientists study ATP to understand how life functions at a molecular level.
